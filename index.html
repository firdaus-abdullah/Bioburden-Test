02Purpose & Scope
Clause 1.0 and 2.0 of SOP QCSC.061.06.
1.0 PURPOSE

To give guideline on microbial bioburden testing method and specification in QC Microbiology Laboratory for Duopharma (M) Sdn Bhd.

KEY CONCEPT · USP <61> Bioburden testing determines the total number of viable micro-organisms in a sample before sterilisation — an essential control for raw materials, in-process samples and bulk solutions.

2.0 SCOPE — sample types

1
Non-Sterile Grade Incoming Raw Material and OSD product with bioburden limit specification
2
In-Process Sterile Product after capping or sealing (SVP and SPF Area)
3
Haemodialysis Concentrated Solution
4
Bulk Solution of Disinfectants After Mixing Stage (Sterile Production)
5
Formulated DP Solution (FDS)
6
Bulk Solution After Mixing Stage in SVP Area
7
Cleaning Verification or Validation sample
03Responsibility & Frequency
Clause 3.0 and 4.0 — clear ownership protects the reliability of results.
3.1

Senior Technician, QC and above

  • Ensure bioburden testing is conducted according to the SOP.
  • Inform area owner and QA Manager if microbial count is out of specification.
  • Review and approve the bioburden report.
3.2

Lab Technician, QC and above

  • Provide sterilised / depyrogenated containers and sterile sampling reagents.
  • Perform bioburden testing per SOP.
  • Immediately inform superior of OOS results.
  • Provide complete results and report at end of testing.
3.3

Operator, Sterile Production

  • Sample the bulk solution of disinfectants after mixing and pass to QC Microbiology.
  • Sample the bulk solution of sterile product after mixing and pass to QC Microbiology.
  • Aseptically sample in-process sterile product after capping or sealing.
3.4

Senior Supervisor, Validation and above

  • Sample the cleaning verification / validation samples.
  • Pass samples to QC Microbiology Laboratory for testing.
4.0 FREQUENCY
As and when required

05Media & Incubation Conditions
The two culture media used across all bioburden tests — know these parameters.

Used for Total Aerobic Microbial Count (TAMC) — detects aerobic bacteria.
Incubation
30 – 35 °C
Duration
5 days
SDACSabouraud Dextrose Agar + Chloramphenicol
Used for Total Yeast and Mold Count (TYMC) — detects fungi.
Incubation
20 – 25 °C
Duration
5 days
Always pair them: Every bioburden test is performed on both TSA and SDAC so that bacteria and fungi are both detected. A negative control must accompany every batch using the same diluent and media.
Diluents used: Buffered Sodium Chloride Peptone Solution (BSPS) pH 7.0 · BSPS with 0.1% w/v Polysorbate 80 for fatty / oily samples · Sterile 0.1% w/v peptone for rinsing membranes.
06Sample Types & Preparation
Clauses 5.1 – 5.6 — click a tab to see the details for each sample category.
5.1CLAUSE

Non-Sterile Grade Incoming Raw Material & OSD Product

Water-soluble: Dissolve / dilute 10 g or 10 mL in sterile BSPS to 10× dilution — further 100× / 1,000× if required per POA.

Non-fatty insoluble: Same as above but using BSPS + 0.1% w/v Polysorbate 80.

Fatty samples: Dissolve 10 g / 10 mL in BSPS + Polysorbate 80 to a total of 100 mL; heat to ≤ 40 °C if needed.

Transfer 1 mL of each dilution into 90 mm Petri dishes → pour plate. Test in duplicate with negative control. Record in F/QC/125.

Pour Plate BSPS ± Polysorbate 80 F/QC/125 Per POA
5.2CLAUSE

Sterile Production In-Process During Filling Stage (SVP & SPF)

SVP / Eyedrops: Sample every 2 hours until completion — at least 5 units per interval (2 for bioburden + 3 for volume).

Prefilled Syringes (PFS): At least 10 units per interval (5 for bioburden + 5 for volume). Pool entire contents in sterile container.

SPF: 5 samples per hour — 2 for bioburden (reconstituted with 10 mL sterile BSPS) + 3 for weigh check.

Membrane filtration for products in Appendix 2 (100 mL per membrane, rinse 3 × 100 mL peptone); Pour plate for others. Test within 1 hour at RT or 2–8 °C up to 24 hours.

Membrane · F/QC/322 Pour Plate · F/QC/099 ≤ 1 h at RT · 2–8 °C ≤ 24 h
5.3CLAUSE

Haemodialysis Concentrated Solution

Aseptically transfer 100 mL of sample into sterile container under laminar airflow.

Transfer 10 mL into depyrogenated bottle for Bacterial Endotoxin Test.

Dilute 10 mL of concentrated solution in 90 mL sterile 0.1% w/v peptone.

Filter the entire content onto 0.45 µm sterile membrane; rinse 3 × 100 mL peptone; transfer to pre-poured TSA.

Membrane Filtration F/QC/258 TAMC < 200 cfu/mL (BP)
5.4CLAUSE

Prepared Bulk Solution of Disinfectant in Sterile Production

Applies to 70% v/v filtered alcohol, 0.2% v/v filtered Benzalkonium and 1% v/v filtered Sodium Hypochlorite.

Sample 50 mL from top and bottom of the mixing tank into 100 mL sterile container. Label and bring to QC Microbiology.

Perform pour-plate method — transfer 1 mL into sterile 90 mm Petri dishes; pour TSA and SDAC. Negative control included.

Pour Plate F/QC/111 Limit < 1 cfu/mL
5.5CLAUSE

Formulated DP Solution (FDS) & Bulk Solution for SVP After Mixing Stage

FDS: Sample in pre-sterilised 1L 2D Single Use Bioprocess bag before filtration — refer to QCSD.001.

SVP: After mixing, sample aseptically from top and bottom of the mixing tank.

Appendix 1 products: at least 100 mL from each (top and bottom). Other SVP & FDS: 10 mL from each.

Membrane filtration for Appendix 1 products; pour plate for others. Test within 1 hour at RT or 2–8 °C up to 24 hours.

Membrane · < 10 cfu/100 mL Pour · < 1 cfu/mL F/QC/111
5.6CLAUSE

Cleaning Verification or Validation Sample

Rinse sample: Collect 250 mL of rinse filtrate from scheduled machine part into sterile container; filter 100 mL through 0.45 µm membrane; transfer to TSA & SDAC.

Swab sample: Pre-wet sterile cotton swab with sterile BSPS; swab a 25 cm² area; transfer into sterile container with 10 mL BSPS; transfer 1 mL into 90 mm Petri dish in duplicate.

Test within 1 hour at RT or 2–8 °C up to 24 hours.

Rinse: < 100 cfu/mL (OSD) Swab: < 50 cfu/25 cm² (OSD) Swab: < 10 cfu/25 cm² (Haemo/Sterile)
Reminder: All sample preparations must be performed under aseptic conditions, and each test must be accompanied by a negative control using the same diluent and media.
